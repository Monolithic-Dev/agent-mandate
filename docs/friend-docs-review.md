## Not adopted

- **Keystone content** (assumptions, decisions, impact scoring, owner routing, extraction pipeline): a different product. Kept only as a documented fallback by the teammate; not mixed in.
- **Replacing the deterministic parser with a model-based extractor as the default.** The teammate also prefers a deterministic parser for demo phrases; an optional model extractor stays behind a flag and is evaluated separately.
- **Reporting scores from their comparison document.** They are the teammate's heuristic, not measurements.

## Things in their documents to double-check before relying on them

- MAP-Graph (arXiv 2608.10509) is described as the closest prior art; its code or paper was not read here. The positioning in [prior-art.md](./prior-art.md) stays conservative.
- Their note that the FalkorDB "Memory Meets Motion" standouts include a governance project is confirmed (AgentCourt Precedent, which uses LLM debate and stores precedent; it does not derive authority from owner utterances).
- Statistics from a vendor survey are context only.
